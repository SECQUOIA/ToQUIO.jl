Bounded classical encoding study: protocol v1

The single accepted campaign completed all 680 planned runs, reserving 5,325,120 proposals. It used the four-family corpus and settings frozen in protocol_v1.toml. The result is exploratory: neither new encoding has an established general advantage. Logical size, coefficient scales and this CPU heuristic do not show physical-device fidelity, universal search fairness or quantum speedup.

Compiler/sampler source: 2a4f1809ebcc148748f57a8fb2772eca3899e1bb. Julia 1.10.11; Float64, one Julia/BLAS thread, an OS one-CPU quota and 4 GiB/no-swap limit. Campaign wall time was 674.175 s; peak process RSS was 827.96 MiB. The raw Project/Manifest and all dependency versions are archived. HiGHS.jl 1.26.0 supplied bounded numerical references; pilots used exhaustive source optima. Numerical solver status/bounds are not exact-arithmetic certificates.

Held-out evaluation

Eight held-out instances (two per family), five planned restarts per arm/regime. Tuning used separate instances and froze cap4 for bounded QUIO and cap1 for the ToQUBO shim. The tuning ledger, scores and costs remain in the raw archive. All runs completed as valid records; 22 evaluation and 3 tuning runs were clock censored and retain partial trajectories. Actual completed proposals were 5,304,189; unspent proposals did not replenish the reserved allowance.

F is any visited source-feasible state, including the initial state, not a penalized-energy or target-domain test. Targets use the independently frozen reference incumbent and sense-aware tolerance. Time score is first timely hit or a 2-second failure penalty; work score is first-hit proposal or the common 10,000-proposal cap. These are failure scores, not raw latency or actual work. Source observations are included in solve timing. Missing hits remain missing.

Generated evaluation tables report every arm/regime's feasibility, target-hit and censoring counts, time/work scores, unpaired conditional gaps and instances-with-gap counts. Unpaired gap sets differ between arms; the paired comparisons use common feasible restart pairs. All eight paired gap differences, counts and missing entries are also generated, rather than copied here.

The separate evaluation.csv includes snapshot feasibility weighted by archived multiplicities, missing latency counts and distinct source snapshots. Snapshot rates have a different observation denominator from F: encodings change sweep lengths and representation multiplicity. Repeated target representations and snapshots are not independent source solutions. All valid best points and archived snapshot feasibility flags were checked against the original sources.

QUIO compiler-default cells intentionally repeat the controlled policy, seeds, initialization and polynomial. Clock stops can differ. They are calibration repeats and are not pooled across regimes as independent evidence. ToQUBO defaults retain actual preprocessing/GCD/linear residuals and constitute a separate compiler-policy comparison. The pinned upstream binary arm and disclosed exact bounded shim are distinct arms; upstream Bounded was not silently patched.

Primary paired contrasts

Candidate minus native, aggregated within instance first. Positive F is better; negative gap/time/work is better. Gap differences use jointly feasible paired restarts; absent pairs remain missing. Below, n is observed instance differences, not sample count. The archive retains every one of the eight differences and paired-restart counts for every endpoint and all secondary contrasts.

Generated contrast tables report every primary and secondary endpoint, its distinct-instance counts, descriptive interval and sensitivity p-value. The standalone page explicitly labels calibration repeats and the absence of multiplicity control.

The protocol uses 2,000 family-stratified instance/restart bootstrap resamples, seed99001, paired restart IDs and percentile intervals. Only two instances per family make these intervals coarse, descriptive and potentially narrow. Sign-flip sensitivity enumerates all 2^n assignments and assumes exchangeable signs; it is not a randomized or confirmatory test. Equal tails use a scale-aware summation roundoff bound, 2n * eps(Float64) * sum(abs, differences), recorded for each endpoint as tie_absolute_tolerance. This corrects the compiler-defaults shim minus binary feasibility sensitivity from 0.5 to 0.625 without changing raw data. Reported means use a fixed left-to-right reduction so processor-dependent SIMD summation cannot change the last bits of reproduced artifacts. There is no multiplicity control. The bounded-native paired signed-gap interval is negative here, while its sensitivity p is 0.0625 with six observed instances. This is the attainable floor here with five nonzero same-sign differences and one zero difference. Facility-3011 contributes a paired-gap estimate from only one jointly feasible restart. The negative interval does not establish general superiority or erase missing pairs and the lower observed feasibility rate.

All primary contrasts against native have eight layout-distinct and exact-target-distinct instances. Radix and bounded differ from each other on only two of eight targets in each regime, the knapsack pair. The other six are aliases, so the similar primary outputs are not independent wins. Identity counts for every contrast/regime and all secondary endpoints are reproduced in the generated tables.

Structural and timing tradeoffs

Means over the eight selected held-out cells in each regime. Couplers combine symmetric contributions after shifts/fixed substitution/binary-square folding; density denominators, coefficient scales and full raw/canonical polynomials are archived. Variable counts are logical, not physical resources. Matrices use dense storage; zero coefficients do not allocate sparse storage.

Generated structural tables provide variables, unique couplers, warm own build time/allocations, canonical minimum-nonzero and maximum coefficient magnitudes, and their ratio, for all arms and regimes. These values are recomputed and compared in CI rather than copied arithmetic.

Warm source/native construction is shared once per instance and has separate fields. Own build includes encoding/backend copies, target extraction and metadata capture; defensive harness deepcopy is recorded separately and excluded. Per-run end-to-end fields sum shared native setup, own build and instrumented solve/observation; do not sum shared setup repeatedly across arms. Queue time is not applicable to this serial local solver. Snapshot/source checking belongs to instrumented solve; report generation and raw artifact persistence are outside those per-run timing fields. Initial fresh-process reporting took 13.552 seconds before the added audits, including startup/JIT, archive/source QA, bootstrap and result writes; its scope and limits are recorded in postprocessing.json. Per-job archive persistence was not separately instrumented. Raw provider-style timing is not invented.

The selected small models fit well below the 4 GiB cap. Warm own builds are milliseconds, and the largest observed allocation cost is the default shim. This does not demonstrate a storage bottleneck requiring a core rewrite. A bounded sparse-storage profiling follow-up is optional for larger future models; this study did not expand sizes or rewrite the compiler after seeing outcomes.

Post-hoc demonstration subset

hardware_subset.json and its generated TOML counterpart retain the lowest-ordinal held-out source per family. This rule was selected post hoc, after the campaign; no pre-evaluation commitment exists. It is a four-family demonstration packet, not an unbiased or representative comparative sample. The selected versus excluded composition is reproduced in the generated tables and stored in the subset record. Adverse feasibility/time-score instance counts, whether selected or excluded, are computed and recorded in the subset and generated tables. Those values expose the current packet's favorable composition. Hardware results on this packet cannot establish a representative advantage. Maintainers and #52 must review its purpose and composition before any #53 approval; changing the subset would be a new explicit frozen decision, not a silent replacement.

Four is the radix/bounded encoding parameter, not the native-domain limit or a verified provider cap. Selected native targets exceed a hypothetical four-level cap; per-arm actual maximum/total levels are generated in TABLES.md. The subset now records per-arm actual maximum/total levels, cell keys/hashes and unresolved provider eligibility. No unsupported domain is hidden. #52 must verify the actual provider contract, and #53 still requires account and manifest/budget approval. D-Wave/IBM remain conditional binary routes.
