CQT encoding study: protocol v1

This is the versioned design for issue #46. It is frozen for human review; integration of this PR accepts v1. The numerical settings live in benchmarks/protocol_v1.toml. A run must archive that file's hash, this document's hash, executable source revisions and its resolved benchmark Manifest before evaluation. This protocol is not a report of an executed campaign.

Questions and supported models

The supplied CQT Year 4 proposal motivates two hypotheses: bounded-domain radix maps may improve quality/time when native integer domains exceed available levels; bounded-weight maps may improve conditioning under limited coefficient precision. Neither predicts a universal advantage. Negative or mixed results satisfy the research milestone when the complete frozen comparison is reported.

The contract covers finite integer/binary domains, integer-valued affine equality and inequality rows, and affine/quadratic objectives. The four-family campaign uses linear objectives, permitting a capable direct MIP reference. Tiny quadratic fixtures validate algebra, not an unplanned nonconvex solver campaign. Continuous discretization, nonlinear constraints and the proposal's other research strands are excluded. Four levels is a computational study setting, not a verified limit of QCI or another device. Live access, device limits and spending remain #53 gates.

Bounded binary comparator correction

The pinned ToQUBO bounded interval source uses a residual interval of width d+r plus r-1 digits of weight mu, where W=r*mu+d. An independent source-formula enumeration finds width3/cap2 maps to 0:2, and width6/cap2 maps to 0:7. Width20/cap2 produces weights [1,2,4,3,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,2], image 0:28 and a weight4 exceeding cap2. For 0<W<mu, the formula also calls its allocator with r-1=-1. This is a latent negative-count request whose runtime effect on the pinned target is unverified, not another demonstrated coverage failure: width1/cap2's arithmetic image is exactly 0:1. Pure-arithmetic fixtures preserve that distinction and the demonstrated width3/6/20 failures without a dependency. This audit is not full compiler execution. It fails the exact box-image contract needed here. The native Bounded method therefore cannot be an unchecked baseline at this pin.

Freeze the arm name toquboboundedshim. #50 owns a small experiment-only ToQUBO interval-method adapter emitting exactly protocol_layout(W; levels=2, max_weight=mu) and offset L. It uses the pinned compiler's binary variable allocator, not Binary on an upstream residual, and returns an affine map without digit-enforcement penalties. Apply it to original integers and slacks. Record adapter revision/hash and distinguish it from upstream Bounded in every result. No vendor source patch, silent alternate pin, or native-method performance claim is allowed. If its actual compile/decode preflight fails, stop the campaign and report the failed arm; do not drop it and call the comparison complete. The shim is ToQUIO-authored and shares a construction with the QUIO bounded arm; it is not an independent encoder. The emitted-map coverage and polynomial gates below protect against common-mode mistakes instead of trusting layout agreement.

Exact affine contract

Let the native full state be z=[x;s], with integer endpoints L=ceil(l), U=floor(u), and W=U-L. Reject an empty interval. Each variable must have a map z_i=L_i+sum(w_ij*d_ij), 0<=d_ij<=m_ij, with integer digits. For every permitted digit state, its image lies in the native domain; every native value has a preimage. Negative offsets are retained. Fixed variables have a constant expansion and no digit; their source values must be restored.

The proposed reference construction starts with coverage C=0. While C<W, choose w=min(C+1,W-C) (also min(w,mu) for bounded), m=min(levels-1,floor((W-C)/w)), and increase C by w*m. Each digit has m+1<=levels values. The inequality w<=C_old+1 prevents gaps between shifted covered intervals; the endpoint sum equals W exactly. A final residual digit can be redundant. Radix uses full powers where they fit, with a bounded tail; it is not a promise of the information-theoretic minimum digit count. At levels2 the unbounded construction reduces to ToQUBO's bounded-tail binary map. Bounding weights alone does not bound final coupler magnitudes: penalties/source terms also contribute. Measure those coefficients rather than claiming a device cap.

Nonunique representations are allowed, and all are decoded identically. They change state multiplicity and local-search neighborhoods; polynomial equivalence does not imply equal sampling distributions. A deterministic right inverse (canonical lift) is required for shared controlled initialization and replay; lift_native_state is authoritative for ToQUIO arms. Experiment adapters and the executable protocol helper use the same decreasing-weight order, with ties in original digit order. Archive the actual ordered maps and initial digit state; its roundtrip must be tested on every tiny native state. Source feasibility reuses #34 after full composed decoding; no rounding, repair or target-energy inference is permitted. Source objective values come from original functions.

For z=a+B*d and P(z)=z'G*z+g'z+gamma, substitute

\[G_d=B^TGB,\quad g_d=B^T(g+(G+G^T)a),\quad \gamma_d=a^TGa+g^Ta+\gamma.\]

This handles nonsymmetric matrix storage. For a canonical MOI quadratic function, a diagonal ScalarQuadraticTerm coefficient c contributes c*z_i^2/2; an off-diagonal term contributes c*z_i*z_j. Thus emit diagonal coefficient 2G_ii and one off-diagonal coefficient G_ij+G_ji, never blindly double every entry. Global source-penalty guarantees carry through the exact map; weak hints remain heuristic. Mathematical substitution does not improve an insufficient penalty.

Compute endpoint/weight/count arithmetic in checked integers or BigInt before allocation. Convert coefficients only with representability checks, and reject nonfinite transformed matrices, linear terms, constants or decoded values. The Float64 campaign uses small exact integer inputs; preflight rejects loss of integer endpoints/weights or failed polynomial parity. A provider roundtrip must also check actual maximum levels and precision. Quantization/normalization/device errors are #52 diagnostics and #53 confounders, not source-map exactness claims.

Generated-instance exactness gates

Tiny level4 pilots use identity maps for the QUIO arms and cannot alone validate multi-digit encoding. Before sampling, #50 must apply the following checks to all 12 generated models, both regimes, all arms and every tuning candidate:

  • Read emitted original/slack expansions and target bounds from ToQUIO metadata or ToQUBO VariableEncodingFunction/SlackVariableEncodingFunction. Require exact integer offsets, weights and digit bounds with no lossy real-ATol path, and compare with the declared layout (identity for native, levels4 radix or bounded, levels2 Binary or shim). Account explicitly for any removed fixed variables/slacks and compiler-default residual scaling.
  • Independently compute reachable sums by dynamic programming on those emitted digits, not by calling the construction again. Require image exactly L+{0,...,W}, with no holes or out-of-domain sums. Limit W to64 before allocation; test the canonical-lift roundtrip for every value of every original and slack interval. This avoids an exponentially large full-state enumeration.
  • Check constant- and sense-inclusive polynomial parity on 64 seeded random digit states per compiled cell, plus canonical lifts of the source witness and frozen reference incumbent with the corresponding exact residual slacks. For preflight RNG use MersenneTwister seed 98001+10000*ordinal+100*arm_index+10*regime_index+cap, taking cap0 for unbounded arms and the listed arm/regime order. These checks are not sampler restarts. Controlled arms compare with the single native polynomial; default arms compare with their own independently reconstructed pre-encoding polynomial, using archived original objective, applied penalties, residual scales, slack domains, removed rows and sense. Defaults must not be compared as if source penalties matched. Use the TOML absolute/relative energy tolerances.

Any missing map, noninteger coefficient, incomplete roundtrip or parity failure stops the full campaign. These gates are required runner work in #50, not a claim that this protocol PR has already executed future encoders. Unit tests extend independent coverage/lifts through width64. Archive gate inputs/results and RNG seeds. Record each instance/arm's normalized offset/weight/digit-bound layout equality with native and radix (order-independent within each native variable); report the number of structurally distinct instances for every contrast. Widths up to3 yield native identity, and cap4 equals radix through width19: zero contrasts on those cells are structural degeneracy, not evidence of an empirical null effect.

Independent references and targets

Exhaustively enumerate original pilot boxes to certify their source optima. Before tuning, solve each of the twelve generated source models once with HiGHS.jl 1.26.0: one thread, seed777, 10 seconds, relative/absolute MIP gap0. Record termination, feasible original incumbent, objective bound with its correct sense, solve time and tolerances. Validate returned values with #34; never round or use a competing encoded arm to choose the reference. Freeze/hash references before any tuning/evaluation. A solver-optimal status is a numerical certificate within its tolerances, not an exact-arithmetic proof. Larger targets are labeled best-known feasible values under this budget even when optimality is reported. Missing/invalid references stop the campaign; do not replace the instances.

With sigma=+1 for minimization and -1 for maximization, report signed source gap sigma*(f-f_ref)/max(1,abs(f_ref)). Positive is worse, negative can improve a best-known reference and must remain visible. The denominator uses one native objective unit near zero. Tiny targets require the exact optimum within absolute 1e-9; generated targets allow gap<=0.01 plus absolute1e-9. The target is fixed independently of all encodings and does not certify a heuristic's optimality.

Controlled Metropolis and budgets

#50 implements the experiment-only single-site integer Metropolis kernel. Each proposal chooses an active digit uniformly and an independent +/-1 with equal probability; out-of-domain proposals reject and still count. Initialize uniformly in the native full box, then canonical-lift the identical native state for controlled arms and the repeated ToQUIO default cells. ToQUBO compiler-default arms share original-variable initialization; compiler-specific auxiliaries use recorded seeded initialization and are a confounder. No source optimum or reference witness seeds the competing samplers. Use separate MersenneTwister streams: initialization seed=effective run seed, proposal seed=effective run seed+1000000000; these seed values are identical across paired arms. Record both identities. Effective run seed=listed replicate seed+10000*instance ordinal from the family-major table. Warmups use replicate seed39001.

Normalize minimization energy as sigma_target*P_target/S, with S=max(1,source objective box range) shared across arms. Read actual compiled sense: a compiler may already negate a maximization objective. Use the geometric schedule from T=2 to 0.01 across the reserved proposal count (T=2 for one proposal), and Metropolis acceptance min(1,exp(-delta_energy/T)). Consume a declared fixed RNG draw sequence per proposal, including rejects, and validate incremental energy changes against full evaluation, boundary rejections and exact tiny-state behavior. Retain +/-1 even for binary digits, as the declared reference kernel requires; one direction is then always invalid. Record per-run boundary rejections, in-domain proposals, accepted moves and energy rejections. Require boundary rejections+accepted moves+energy rejections=completed work, and in-domain proposals=accepted moves+energy rejections. The binary arms' boundary waste is an explicit confounder. Replay an interrupted run using the original reserved proposal count for its temperature schedule, stopping at the archived completed-work count; seeds alone cannot reproduce a wall-clock stop, and shortening the schedule changes the path. This is a local-search control, not a physical QCI simulator.

A sweep is Nactive proposals. Schedule `min(proposalcap,sweepcap*Nactive)`; the equal caps below make the proposal count equal across nonempty arms. Stop also at the 2-second per-run limit, checked every proposal, or a global ledger limit. Fixed targets are evaluated once without a proposal. Warm each family/arm/regime once on pilot data before measured timings; warmups consume the same ledger but never contribute measured outcomes.

Endpoints, failures and statistics

For every visited state, decode and check the original model to observe first target attainment and the best feasible objective; include this observation cost in measured solve latency. A visited state is the initialization plus the current chain state after each proposal's accept/reject decision. Rejected candidates are not observed states; repeated chain states retain their multiplicity. Use monotonic time_ns: start immediately before initialization and canonical lift, after compilation/warmup, and stop after the final observation. First-hit latency includes initialization/lift and decoding; timely hits require the observation to finish within2s. This is an instrumented sampler latency, not bare kernel/device latency. Retain initial-state target hits separately with proposal index0. Record sample observations after each complete sweep and the final partial sweep; rejected/duplicate states retain multiplicity and ordering. Read counts are not independent experimental replicates. No-feasible-state runs have missing gap, not zero. Record first feasible/target time, proposal index, actual elapsed time, completed work, termination and censoring flags; unavailable timings stay missing.

Primary feasibility is F_imr=1 iff planned run r visits at least one original- model-feasible state (including initialization), else0. Estimate it per instance/ arm as sum(F_imr)/5 over all five planned evaluation restarts. Final-state feasibility and the correlated per-sweep feasible-read fraction are secondary diagnostics, with explicit sample counts; they cannot replace this endpoint. Primary quality also includes signed best-feasible gap with its missingness count. Primary time output is first-target latency with failures plus a capped target-time score: observed first hit, or the common 2-second failure penalty if no hit. The score is not actual spent time when a proposal cap ended early and is not a 99%-reliability claim. Report raw censored observations and target-success fractions alongside it; zero successes remain zero with all raw target times missing and score2, never fabricated finite hits. Hits observed after the run deadline do not count as timely successes and are retained as overshoot diagnostics. Missing timings invalidate the corresponding time comparison, not the quality data when source observations remain validated. Also report proposals to first target (initial hit0), raw no-hit proposal censoring and a capped work score: first-hit index or the common stage proposal cap for no timely hit. The work score is not actual spent work; record the smaller actual work at clock censoring. It supplies a common work penalty alongside uneven wall censoring and exposes observation overhead; the 2s time score can largely track the success indicator and must not be called a pure speed metric.

A run interrupted by its ordinary 2s cap is a valid partial trajectory: its validated feasible visits/gap remain, and an absent timely target receives score2. A crash, corrupted/missing trajectory or unverifiable decoding is an invalid planned run: assign F=0, target failure, time score2, work score equal to its stage cap and gap missing, even if a partial file suggests a hit. Count and describe these separately; no rerun or selective deletion is allowed. Missing clock data invalidate raw latency comparisons, not validated quality; show the missing count and mark any affected time-score contrast incomplete rather than silently dropping its replicate. A preflight-refused cell, global quota exhaustion or externally interrupted campaign is an incomplete campaign with no completed- campaign aggregate; preserve partial records and the reason, never relabel it a sampler failure or report a completed result. All planned cells must be accounted for before a complete exploratory report is allowed. The same F, invalid-run, ordinary clock-censoring, target-time and work-score rules apply to all120 tuning starts, with five planned replicates per instance/arm/cap cell. Select caps using the all-five denominator, including invalid failures; a preflight/global failure instead leaves tuning incomplete and stops evaluation.

Structural outputs include allocated original/native/encoded integer and binary counts, each domain's levels, active variables, build time, allocated bytes and observed peak resident memory. For coefficient/coupler comparisons, use a common canonical polynomial: shift active coordinates by their lower endpoints, substitute fixed coordinates into the constant/linear terms, and fold each binary-coordinate square into its linear term. Report nonbinary square, linear and unique off-diagonal coefficients G_ij+G_ji separately; the constant is archived but excluded from coefficient ratios. This avoids comparing storage conventions or duplicate terms as physical couplers. Density is 2*couplers/(N_active*(N_active-1)) (0 for N_active<2). Report max/min-nonzero absolute coefficients and their ratio (missing if no nonzero coefficients). Archive both raw compiled and canonical matrices/offsets so this normalization can be replayed. Time warm source construction, reformulation, encoding/backend copying, and solve/observation separately; report their sum for end-to-end cost.

Predeclare primary controlled contrasts radix-native and bounded-native. Binary-vs-native, bounded-binary-vs-binary and compiler-default contrasts are secondary. Aggregate repeat outcomes within each instance first; instances are the cross-instance unit. Report paired instance differences, stratified by family, and 95% percentile cluster bootstrap intervals with 2000 resamples/seed99001. Resample instances within each family, retaining paired method records and resampling paired restart IDs within selected instances. Pair methods by the same instance and listed replicate seed. For gaps, compute run-level differences only where both F=1 and both gaps are validated, then the per-instance mean over those pairs, reporting its count (0 means missing). Report all eight paired instance differences, including missing entries. Feasibility/time/work-score differences use each arm's mean over all five planned runs, including the failure rules above. Bootstrap gap statistics retain this pairing and missingness rule. Label the percentile intervals descriptive: two instances per family produce coarse resampling and potentially narrow intervals, not reliable confirmatory uncertainty. Also report the two-sided paired sign-flip sensitivity p-value for each contrast/endpoint by enumerating all2^n sign assignments of its n observed instance differences and counting abs(mean(flipped))>=abs(mean(observed)); n=8 gives256 assignments, gaps may have n<8 (n=0 means missing). This diagnostic assumes exchangeable signs, is not a randomized experiment test, and is not confirmatory. There is no multiplicity control across two contrasts and at least three primary endpoints. Describe gap comparisons as conditional and do not hide failure rates. Eight evaluation instances/five repeats support a bounded exploratory result, not a broad superiority or quantum-speedup claim.

Archive effective seeds, run order (shuffle seed99011), schedule/work/timing boundaries, source parameters/models, exact matrices/decoders/penalties, snapshots, all raw sample multiplicities, references, statuses, errors, locks and dependency/source hashes. Record solver/precision/neighborhood/multiplicity differences as confounders. Any protocol change requires a new version/manifest before evaluation; preserve old evidence instead of tuning mandatory scope after seeing results.

Tiny worked example and implementation gates

The knapsack pilot maximizes 3x+2y with 2x+y<=3, integer x in0:2, y in0:1. Its exact source optimum is 5 at (1,1). Slack is0:3 and default magnitude is9. The explicit binary map x=d1+d2,y=d3,s=d4+2d5 maps32 digit states onto24 native states with duplicate representations, preserving every polynomial value. An illustrative magnitude0.25 instead gives target optimum 7 at source-infeasible (2,1). That is an override-risk example, not an automatic compiler defect.
